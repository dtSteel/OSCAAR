About OSCAAR

OSCAAR — Open Source Cancer Analysis and Research — exists to put the world's cancer literature within reach of the people who need it: the clinicians treating cancer, the researchers studying it, and the patients and families living with it.

Roughly 18 to 20 people die from cancer every minute worldwide. Behind that number is an enormous and fast-growing body of research — millions of peer-reviewed studies — that holds answers, but is scattered across journals and databases in language that's often impenetrable to anyone outside the field. OSCAAR was built to bridge that gap.

How it works

OSCAAR draws on a curated library of millions of peer-reviewed cancer articles. You ask a question in plain language — the way you'd ask a colleague — and OSCAAR answers directly from that literature, citing every source so you always know where the answer came from. Nothing is invented; every response is grounded in published research, and answers are tailored to whether you're a clinician seeking technical depth or a patient seeking clear, accessible explanations.

Why open source

OSCAAR is built in the open because cancer knowledge shouldn't sit behind walls. The platform is freely available, and its development is transparent. Our aim is a resource that serves the global community working against cancer — not a product that serves shareholders.

OSCAAR is a research and information tool. It summarizes and cites the published literature, but it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Decisions about care should always be made with a qualified healthcare professional who knows your specific situation.
